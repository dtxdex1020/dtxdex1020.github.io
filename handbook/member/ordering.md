# Ordering

Custom work starts in `#order`. After you submit the form, a private ticket opens in that department. A designer reviews the request there and discusses a quote with you.

## Steps

1. In `#order`, open the menu labeled **Choose a service** and pick one.
2. On the payment menu, pick **PayPal** or **Robux**.
3. Complete the short form with your budget, the deadline you prefer, the specs, and any reference links that help.
4. Keep talking in the private department ticket that opens from the form.
5. Stay in that ticket until you and the studio have settled the scope, the price, how you will pay, and what you will receive. Work should not move ahead of that agreement.

## Services

Choose the closest match:

- Clothing
- Graphics
- Discords
- Liveries
- Lighting/ELS
- Photography
- Bot Development

Not sure which service fits? Ask `#assistance` before you submit. Keep a single request together instead of filing extras.

## Custom work and ready-made packages

A custom commission is built around your brief, or changed in a substantial way for you. Ready-made packages are products that already exist in the package forums. Read what the listing says you get, and how it is delivered, before you buy. If you need a large change, use `#order` instead of the package flow.

Stay on **Choose a service** in `#order`. Skip any older form that wants you to write the department in by hand.
