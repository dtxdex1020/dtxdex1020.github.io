# Server Rules

## Respect

Be decent to everyone in the server, including other clients, the staff, and the people making the work. Say what you need clearly, and use the channel meant for that kind of request.

## Tickets

- Keep one request in one ticket. If you are not sure which channel fits, ask in `#assistance` before you open another.
- Do not ping staff over and over. Wait for the reply in the ticket.
- If you disagree with a decision, take it to `#assistance`. Do not turn a public channel into a fight.

## Not allowed

<div class="warn">

Do not harass people, make dishonest claims about an order or a payment, or misuse files the studio delivered.

</div>

That includes selling the files on, posting them publicly, copying them out to other people, or letting another community use them. The [Payments](member/payments.md) page states what a purchase allows. If a use might sit outside what you agreed, ask in the ticket first.

## Public channels

Keep passwords, account cookies, authentication codes, and private payment details out of public chat. Order facts belong in the ticket.
