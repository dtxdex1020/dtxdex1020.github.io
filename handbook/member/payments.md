# Payments

## Accepted methods

You can pay with **PayPal** or **Robux**. No other payment platform is accepted for studio orders.

The price and the way you will pay are settled in the ticket. Finished files go out only after the studio has confirmed that payment.

## Refunds and chargebacks

Finished work is not refunded.

Opening a chargeback can put you on a network blacklist.

When an order or a delivery goes wrong, bring the ticket history and the details that matter to `#assistance`. Keep that discussion in the ticket, not in a public channel.

## Licensing

What you receive is for use inside your own community.

Buying the work does not allow you to:

- Sell the files on to someone else
- Leak them
- Hand out copies
- Give them to a different community

If you want to use the work in a way the ticket never covered, ask in that ticket before you do it.

## What to keep out of chat

Do not post payment logins, card details, passwords, or similar secrets. The ticket only needs the confirmation that the agreed payment went through.
