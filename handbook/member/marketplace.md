# Marketplace

`#marketplace` is the studio store. It is where you will find:

- Promotions and sponsored giveaways
- Studio Membership
- Donations
- Booster rewards

**Claim My Purchase** and **Promotion Schedule** are the controls for claims and the promotion calendar. This handbook does not list prices.

## Ready-made packages

Ready-made packages live in the package forums. Open the information card in `#shop` to reach them.

A package is an existing product with its own listing and delivery details. Read those before you buy. A custom commission is a project shaped around your brief. If you need something made for you, or changed in a substantial way, use `#order` instead.

## If something goes wrong

Open `#assistance` and name the product, promotion, or claim involved. Do not send payment passwords or account credentials.
