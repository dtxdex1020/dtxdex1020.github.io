# FAQ

**Can I pay with something other than PayPal or Robux?**  
No. Studio orders only accept PayPal or Robux.

**When are files delivered?**  
Finished files go out after the studio confirms payment. By then the ticket should already record what you are getting, the price, how you are paying, and how delivery works.

**Can I get a refund?**  
Finished work is not refunded. A chargeback can put you on a network blacklist. If the order or the delivery is a problem, take the record to `#assistance`.

**Which service should I pick?**  
In `#order`, choose the closest match: Clothing, Graphics, Discords, Liveries, Lighting/ELS, Photography, or Bot Development. If you are still stuck, ask in `#assistance` instead of filing the same idea more than once.

**What is the difference between a package and a custom order?**  
Packages in the package forums are products the studio already offers. A custom order starts in `#order` and follows your brief. A large change to a package belongs in `#order`. See [Ordering](member/ordering.md).

**Verification did not finish.**  
Use the panel in `#verify` and follow the provider's steps. If it still fails, open `#assistance`. Never send a password, cookie, or authentication code.

**Where do I review?**  
After the work arrives, post through the panel in `#reviews`. Name the person who made it and the service, and do not paste ticket-only notes into the review. See [Reviews](member/reviews.md).

**How do I apply to the team?**  
Open `#opportunities` and use the Staff Application or Creative Application button. See [Applications](member/applications.md).

**Where are courses?**  
Courses run on their own server: https://discord.gg/DYxRPEGPF. If that invite is dead, ask `#assistance` for a working one.

**Can I resell or share what I bought?**  
No. The files are for your own community. Do not sell them on, post them publicly, pass out copies, or let another community use them. If a use was never written into the ticket, ask there before you do it. See [Payments](member/payments.md).

**Who do I contact?**  
Order help starts in `#assistance`. You can also reach Dex at **sharkmessi1020@gmail.com**.
