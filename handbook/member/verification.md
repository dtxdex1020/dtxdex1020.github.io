# Verification

Link your Roblox account in `#verify` before you depend on studio tools that need it.

## Steps

1. Open `#verify`.
2. Use the verification panel.
3. Follow the verification provider's own instructions to connect your Roblox identity.
4. Finish the flow in Discord.

## What not to send

Staff should never receive your password, an account cookie, or an authentication code. Those are not part of verification.

## If it stops

Open `#assistance` and say which step stalled and what you had already clicked. Leave login secrets out of that note.
