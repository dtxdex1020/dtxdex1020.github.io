# Guidelines

## Clear communication

Use readable spelling and punctuation in tickets. Keep a calm, neutral tone, especially when a client is upset. Avoid sarcasm and vague answers.

## Respect

Treat members and other staff with respect. Do not argue, insult, or provoke. Follow instructions from higher ranks.

## Accountability

Follow studio procedures. If you make a mistake, correct it honestly in the ticket and tell a high rank when needed.

## Escalation

Escalate anything you cannot resolve from this handbook or the ticket evidence. Do not guess at refunds, payouts, or bans.

## Confidentiality

Do not share:

- Internal staff chats
- Application answers
- Payment proof beyond what staff need
- Moderation history
- Private ticket contents in public channels

## Channel discipline

- New custom work → `#order`
- Help / issues / disputes → `#assistance`
- Verification → `#verify`
- Reviews → `#reviews`
- Applications → `#opportunities` buttons (submissions land in staff/creative application channels)
