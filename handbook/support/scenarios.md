# Scenarios

## Escalate to a high rank

Escalate when the ticket involves:

- Marketplace listing problems
- Member reports and serious rule breaks
- Payouts and claims
- Bot / panel bugs
- Appeals and sensitive application decisions
- Refunds or chargebacks
- Threats, harassment, or doxxing attempts

## Rude client

Remain professional. If the member is clearly disrespectful and will not stop, end the productive part of the ticket, close or pause as appropriate, and get a higher rank for a warning if it is severe enough.

## Client asks for a refund

Collect order ID, designer, payment method, and what went wrong. Do not promise a refund. Escalate.

## Client wants a custom job in a help ticket

Send them to `#order` so the department ticket and payment picker run correctly.

## Verification stuck

Send them to `#verify`. Remind them never to give staff a password, cookie, or auth code. If the provider flow fails, gather screenshots and escalate.

## Wrong payment method

State that only PayPal and Robux are accepted. Help them restart the order flow if needed.
