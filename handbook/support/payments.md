# Payments

## What clients may use

**PayPal and Robux only.** If a client offers another method, redirect them to one of those two.

## What support should do

- Confirm the ticket states the agreed method and amount before telling a client payment is settled.
- Keep payment proof and confirmation notes in the ticket.
- Do not release or instruct designers to release final files until payment is confirmed under studio process.
- Never ask for passwords, cookies, or authentication codes.

## Studio shares (reference)

Designers and packers are paid from bot-tracked shares:

| Work type | Creator / packer share | Studio share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom commission (default) | 85% | 15% |
| Package sale | 90% | 10% |

Custom commissions fall back to a **15%** studio cut when no per-order cut is set. Package sales use **90%** packer / **10%** studio. Support should not recalculate payouts by hand for clients; escalate payout questions to staff who run the payout tools.

## Refunds and chargebacks

Completed work is treated as **non-refundable** in the client handbook. Chargebacks can lead to a network blacklist. Support does not approve refunds alone — escalate with the full ticket record.
