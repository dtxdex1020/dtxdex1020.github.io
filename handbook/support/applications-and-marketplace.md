# Applications & Marketplace

## Applications

Staff and Creative applications start from the buttons in **`#opportunities`**.

- Staff submissions land in **`#staff-applications`**
- Creative submissions land in **`#creative-applications`**

When reviewing:

- Read the full answers and portfolio links.
- Do not decide alone on edge cases — follow the hiring process Dex sets with high ranks.
- Keep application contents private.

## Marketplace

`#marketplace` is the studio store area for promotions, sponsored giveaways, Studio Membership, donations, and booster rewards (plus claim / schedule controls as configured in the bot).

- Do not invent product prices or listings.
- Custom commissions still go through `#order`.
- Ready-made packages stay on their package listings / forums.
- Marketplace bugs, failed claims, or suspicious purchases → escalate.
