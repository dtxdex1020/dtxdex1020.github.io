# Payments & Payouts

## Accepted client payment methods

**PayPal and Robux are the only accepted methods.** Do not accept other platforms for studio orders.

Agree payment in the ticket. Release final files only after payment is confirmed according to studio process.

## Studio shares (from the bot)

These rates reflect the current bot defaults. Always check the order record when preparing a payout.

| Work type | Creator / packer share | Studio share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom commission (default) | 85% | 15% |
| Package sale | 90% | 10% |

Notes:

- Custom commissions use the order's configured server-cut percentage, falling back to **15%** when none is set (`server_cut_percent || 15`).
- Package sales credit the packer/designer **90%** of the Robux price; the studio keeps **10%**. Whole-Robux amounts may be rounded by the bot.
- Do not invent a different cut for a client-facing quote unless Dex has approved it for that order.

## Completed work

Completed deliverables are **non-refundable** under the studio client handbook. Chargebacks can lead to a network blacklist. If a client disputes quality or delivery, keep evidence in the ticket and escalate through `#assistance` / high rank rather than arguing publicly.

## Payout status

Orders track payout status in the bot (for example unpaid vs paid). Use the studio payout tools and staff process; do not mark yourself paid outside that system.
