# Leave of Absence

If you will be unavailable for a stretch of time, tell staff before tickets pile up on you.

## What to do

1. Finish or hand off active tickets when possible.
2. Tell a high-ranking staff member (and Dex if needed) how long you expect to be away.
3. Do not claim new work you cannot finish.
