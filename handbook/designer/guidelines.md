# Guidelines

## Communication

- Write clearly in tickets. Prefer complete sentences over slang when discussing price, scope, or delivery.
- Stay calm when a client is frustrated. Do not match hostility.
- Do not promise deadlines, refunds, free extras, or payment changes that you have not agreed with the responsible staff member.

## Respect

Treat clients, support staff, and other designers with respect. Do not argue in public channels. Disagreements go through the ticket or a private staff channel.

## Accountability

Own mistakes. If you miss a detail, say so in the ticket and correct the plan. Do not hide problems until delivery day.

## Confidentiality

Do not share:

- Client payment details beyond what the ticket already records
- Internal staff discussions
- Unpublished assets or other clients' files
- Application contents or moderation notes

## Ticket hygiene

- Keep the quote, agreed scope, payment confirmation, and delivery notes in the same ticket.
- Do not move the conversation to DMs for the core agreement. DMs make the studio lose its record.
- Ask support to help when the client needs verification, a package purchase path, or a general help ticket instead of an order ticket.
