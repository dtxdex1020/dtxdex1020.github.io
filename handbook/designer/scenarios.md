# Scenarios

## Escalate to high rank

Escalate when the ticket involves:

- Refunds or chargebacks
- Payout disputes or missing credits
- Bot / panel failures
- Marketplace listing problems
- Abuse, threats, or harassment
- Scope fights that cannot be settled from the written quote
- Anything that would change studio policy

## Client is rude

Stay professional. If the client is clearly disrespectful and will not stop, stop engaging on the insult, close or pause with a short note, and get a higher rank involved for moderation if needed.

## Client changes the brief mid-project

Restate the new scope, quote the delta, and wait for agreement. Do not silently absorb large extras.

## Payment not confirmed

Do not release final files. Ask the client for the confirmation details the studio uses, and involve support if proof is unclear.

## Wrong payment method mentioned

Remind the client that only PayPal and Robux are accepted. Offer to continue only on one of those methods.
