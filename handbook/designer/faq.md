# FAQ

**Can I take payment outside PayPal or Robux?**  
No. Studio orders only use PayPal or Robux.

**A client wants a refund.**  
Do not approve refunds on your own. Completed work is treated as non-refundable in the client handbook. Escalate with the ticket record.

**The ticket is in the wrong department.**  
Ask support or a high rank to move it. Do not redesign the whole process in chat.

**Where do package sales pay out?**  
Package sales use the packer share tracked by the bot (90% to the packer/designer by default). Use the payout tools; do not calculate privately and DM yourself paid.

**Client wants to talk only in DMs.**  
Bring the agreement back into the ticket. The studio needs the record.

**I do not know how to handle a situation.**  
Check [Scenarios](designer/scenarios.md), then ping a colleague or high rank. Do not invent policy.
