# Quoting & Scope

## Before you quote

Confirm:

1. Service type matches the ticket category
2. Payment method is **PayPal** or **Robux**
3. Budget, deadline, and references are readable
4. The client understands what is included and what is not

## Writing the quote

Put the quote in the ticket so support and Dex can see it later. Include:

- What will be delivered (files, formats, counts)
- What is out of scope
- Total price and payment method
- Expected turnaround after payment is confirmed
- Revision policy for this order (keep it simple and explicit)

Do not start major production until the client has agreed to the quote in the ticket.

## Scope changes

If the client adds work after agreement:

1. Pause and restate the new scope
2. Quote the change
3. Wait for agreement before continuing

Silent scope creep creates payout and refund disputes.

## Deadlines

Only commit to dates you can meet. If you will miss a deadline, tell the client and a high rank early, with a new realistic date.
